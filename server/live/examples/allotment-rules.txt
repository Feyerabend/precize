Hollow Lane Allotment Society: Rules for Plot Holders
(A fictional text, written for the live page of "Who saw what". Any likeness to a real
society is chance.)

1. Plots
1.1 Each plot is let to one member for one season at a time, from 1 March.
1.2 A member shall cultivate at least two thirds of the plot in each season.
1.3 Paths between plots are common ground and shall be kept clear.

2. Structures
2.1 A member may put up one shed and one greenhouse on the plot.
2.2 No structure shall be higher than 2.5 metres.

3. Animals
3.1 Members shall not keep animals or poultry on their plot unless the committee agrees.
3.2 Bees may be kept by members who hold a beekeeping certificate.
3.3 Dogs brought onto the site shall be kept on a lead.

4. The committee
4.1 The committee is elected at the annual meeting and meets once a month.
4.2 A decision of the committee is recorded in its minutes.
