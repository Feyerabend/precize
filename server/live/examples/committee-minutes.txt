Hollow Lane Allotment Society: Committee Minutes, extracts
(A fictional text, written for the live page of "Who saw what".)

Meeting of 4 April
Item 3. Plot 14 asked whether two rabbits in a hutch count as animals under rule 3.1.
The committee noted that rule 3.1 was written in 1998 after a complaint about a goat.
Agreed: the committee does not need to be asked about rabbits kept in hutches.

Meeting of 2 May
Item 2. Plot 9 has kept six hens since March without asking. Two members said the rule
forbids poultry outright, whatever the committee says; the chair said the committee may
agree to poultry as to any other animal. No decision was reached.
Item 5. Plot 21 asked to keep a goat. Refused.

Meeting of 6 June
Item 1. The chair read a letter from Plot 9: "We read rule 3.1 as covering animals only
with the committee's leave, and poultry not at all, so we did not think to ask about
hens." The committee agreed to put the wording of rule 3.1 to the annual meeting.
