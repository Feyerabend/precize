An audit where agents stop and people decide

  
A sentence turns on a word, and two careful readers take the word two
  ways. Agents built on language models can do much of the work of finding that out. They
  quote the sentence, mark the word, list its readings, look things up and propose what
  kind of disagreement it is. Some steps they may not take. To say who holds a
  reading is a claim about people. To settle what a law means is a judgement. Those are
  left to a person, and here the person is you.

Choose a case
Each case is a real audit from the trials in
      the paper: a statute, a court case, a sentence from an AI answer. Two agent runs,
      blind to each other, audited it.

      entry, with what each kind of entry means. The agents stop at the line.

Take your turn
Say who holds a reading, which reading you go
      by, or that you cannot tell. Give your own verdict. The record refuses what breaks its
      rules, and says why.

    
4
Read the report
What stands, what fell, and what is open is
      computed from the entries, yours included. Save it as a PDF and keep your signed log.

  


  
How the record works

  
    
    
  • Each author writes to their own log. An agent run is an author; so are you.
      An entry is never edited. To take something back you write a withdrawal.
    • 
    
  • Every entry names what it rests on. When an entry is withdrawn, what rests
      on it falls with it, and the report shows the chain.
    • 
    
  • Some acts need a person. Holder, grant, settle and decide are refused from an
      agent. An agent's verdict is only a proposal.
    • 
    
  • The report is computed, never stored. Two claims by different authors about
      the same thing, with different values, are open until a person settles them.
    • 
    
  • Entries are signed. This page makes you a key (Ed25519) in your browser and
      signs each entry you write. The key never leaves the page.
    • 
  

  
Nothing you write leaves your browser. If you reload the page, your key and
  your entries are gone, so download your log before you leave. The entries and the report
  are the same as the server's: a log written here loads in the server, signatures and all.
