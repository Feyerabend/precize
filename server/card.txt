The procedure on one sheet

From Saying What You Mean (Lonnert 2026), Appendix A.

0  Write T₀ exactly as uttered. Quotation marks. No repairs.
   No exact wording → stop. You have no object.

1  Mark the vague term.
   Test: could two speakers who know every fact still disagree?
   Then, of the sentence: holding every word to one sense,
   can it still be taken two ways?  If so, mark the sentence
   and ask which reading was meant — the cheapest repair here.
   Nothing marked → the disagreement is about the world. Stop.

1b Vary the term. Discrete readings → take all of them.
   Continuum → both extremes, and the case at hand.
   Never confine yourself to readings you find plausible.
   True under all      → vagueness is idle. Stop; look
                          elsewhere.
   False under all     → T₀ is false on EVERY reading.
                          Stop, and report it in that form.
                          Try it on your OBJECTION too, not
                          just truth. Holds throughout →
                          your objection is not about
                          the vagueness.
   Truth value varies  → continue.

2  Precize the TERM, not the sentence.
       P₁ ≺ w , P₂ ≺ w          then    val(T₀ | P₁) , val(T₀ | P₂)
   Never P ≺ T₀.  Narrow it where it stands, in T₀.

3  Write each reading out in full.
   Instance test: what would you count as an instance?
   Explanation test: can you explain it step by step?
   Clerk's test: could a clerk apply it tomorrow,
     asking you nothing?
   Then the filter: WHO reads it this way? Name nobody → off
   the list.  Two readings alone license no verdict.

4  Two vague terms → grid, not list. Enumerate all four cells
   BEFORE looking at which are occupied. The two positions in
   circulation are usually a diagonal.

5  Name the property, as a question about one case.
   No property → constitutive → no divergence here. Look at the
   proxy term standing next to it.

6  Look for: rule applied FAITHFULLY and CORRECTLY, verdict about
   the property WRONG. Nothing less counts.
   Ask the parties which reading each goes by. Do not guess.

7  Verdict, one of five, in a sentence, with the case:
   1a pseudo-disagreement    1b pseudo-agreement
   2 real disagreement   3 equivocation
   4 rule–property divergence   5 pseudo-alternatives
   None of the five fits → say so and describe what you have.
   The list is open. §6.1 found a precization hollowed out
   rather than repealed, and none of the five fit it.
   No case → print the frame and say the finding is absent.
   Every verdict ends: on what has been said so far.

Then the argument the sentence was a premise in:  N ⊕ F ⇒ A

1  Quote A exactly, in the arguer's words.
2  Recover N. The WEAKEST one that delivers A, not the most
   attackable. Often decided here. Get it GRANTED before you
   attack what follows: am I right that you rely on this?
3  Precize N and F separately. Run steps 0-7 above on each.
4  Drift? F true under its own precization — does it satisfy
   N's antecedent under N's?
5  Quantifiers? Some in the evidence, all in the conclusion?
6  Decorative-premise test: would the conclusion change if F
   were false? If no, stop auditing facts and argue the norm.
7  Scope? Is F established of the thing A acts on?
8  Means? Is A one instrument among several N does not rank?
9  Name which check failed. Then repair it if you can.

And before you publish, the six norms:
   1 Quote what you are answering.
   2 Say which reading you are attacking.
   3 Name the property before arguing about the rule.
   4 Do not offer two readings as if they were all.
   5 Say what would change your mind, before you look.
   6 Say who pays for the precision you are demanding.

The symbols on this sheet, in English:
   ⟦T⟧         every reading T could be given, taken together
   ext(P)      the cases reading P covers. Not the same object
   P ≺ w       P is a precization of w — fewer readings, no new ones
   P₁ ⊥ P₂     incompatible. ext(P₁) ∩ ext(P₂) = ∅, that is:
               nothing counts as both. ∩ is what two collections
               share, ∅ is the collection with nothing in it
   val(T₀|P)   the truth value of T₀, read under P
   N ⊕ F ⇒ A   norm plus fact, neither droppable, therefore action
